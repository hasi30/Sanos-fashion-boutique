How we began

Stitched with patience, worn with love

A small story of two milestones — and every dress in between.

2020 — We began online

Sanos Fashion Boutique started as a home-based online store, taking orders through Instagram and stitching every piece with care for customers across Trichy.

2021 — We opened our shop

As love for our work grew, we opened our very own boutique on Wireless Road, Airport, Trichy — bringing stitching, Aari work and ready-made collections together in one warm little space.

Today

We continue to serve our Trichy family with custom stitching, hand Aari embroidery, and a growing collection of hijabs, abayas, sarees and everyday essentials.
